Glossary & data labels
KYC tiers
Know your customer: identity checks. Retail KYC is basic onboarding; accredited/professional and qualified purchaser tiers add financial eligibility rules; institutions-only excludes individual retail investors. Requirements vary by jurisdiction.
Admitted
At least one investor tier from that region can apply. This does not mean every resident qualifies.
Qualified purchaser (QP)
A higher financial eligibility category than accredited investor. Check the issuer’s jurisdiction-specific requirements.
Wrapper vs blended
A wrapper is one issuer’s tokenized version of an asset. Its premium or discount is measured against CMC’s blended tokenized average, not the underlying stock-exchange price.
Basis-trade
A strategy seeking the price or funding difference between spot assets and derivatives. Returns can fall or reverse.
Live / approx / snapshot
Live means a provider reading captured within 48 hours, not a streaming quote. Approx means an estimate or target. Snapshot means a dated record, or a provider reading older than 48 hours or without a usable date. Sources refresh daily; none of these labels guarantees returns.
Unavailable / not covered
Unavailable means the value or feed could not be obtained. Not covered means a successful CMC category response reported no assets. A dash is never a zero yield.
